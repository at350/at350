## Selected work

Four team projects below live under teammates' accounts. GitHub currently lists me as the top contributor by commit count on each.

- [TR-Benchmarking](https://github.com/at350/tr-benchmarking): A legal LLM evaluation pipeline developed in Northwestern's CS+Law Innovation Lab with Thomson Reuters. It structures answers in IRAC, clusters reasoning with instruction-tuned embeddings, and stress-tests results with adversarial examples. The workflow reduced required expert review from 400 cases to 40 per benchmark.
- [LaborLens](https://github.com/Inika-Goyal/UncommonHacks): A company or region query becomes a cited labor-risk report through a seven-agent LangGraph swarm. The system persists evidence in Supabase and maps findings in an interactive dashboard. Social Impact finalist at Uncommon Hacks.
- [GreenChain](https://github.com/thejustjim/hackprinceton2026): A product, candidate countries, and transport mode become a ranked, cited comparison of real manufacturers. Dedalus agents find suppliers, while XGBoost quantile regression scores environmental cost and uncertainty. Winner of three sponsor tracks at HackPrinceton.
- [Architec](https://github.com/sarakhan7/wildhacks): Twelve months of utility bills become a weather-normalized building energy audit with ranked upgrades, projected savings, payback, and a 3D digital twin. Third of 70 projects at WildHacks and first in the Appifex AI track.
- [Arrival](https://github.com/z1ros/nori): Foreign rent history, bank statements, and domestic cash flow become a portable credit profile issued as an Apple Wallet pass. First place at ALI Builds.
